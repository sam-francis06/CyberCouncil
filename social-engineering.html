    What is Social Engineering?

    Social engineering is the psychological manipulation of people into performing actions or divulging confidential information. It's a type of security attack that relies on human interaction and often involves tricking people into breaking normal security procedures.

    Common Tactics

    • Urgency: Creating time pressure to force quick decisions
    • Authority: Impersonating figures of authority
    • Scarcity: Implying limited availability
    • Social Proof: Suggesting others have complied
    • Familiarity: Pretending to know you or someone you know
    • Fear: Using threats or warnings
    • Greed: Offering rewards or opportunities

    Protection Tips

    • Verify requests through official channels
    • Never provide sensitive information in response to unsolicited communications
    • Be suspicious of urgent requests
    • Check email addresses carefully
    • Don't click on suspicious links
    • Use multi-factor authentication
    • Keep software and systems updated
