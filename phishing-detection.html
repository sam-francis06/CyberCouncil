How to Identify Phishing Attempts

Check URLs Carefully

Look for misspellings, unusual subdomains, or non-standard TLDs. Hover over links before clicking to see the actual destination.

Verify Security

Ensure websites use HTTPS. Look for the padlock icon in your browser's address bar before entering sensitive information.

Be Wary of Urgency

Phishing attempts often create a false sense of urgency. Be suspicious of messages demanding immediate action.

Verify Sender Identity

Check email headers and sender addresses carefully. Contact organizations directly through official channels if you're unsure.
